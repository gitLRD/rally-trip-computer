First release.

* Two independent trip meters plus a live speed readout
* Distance, average speed, trip time and maximum speed
* Regularity mode: stopwatches replace average speed, and switching
  mode clears everything in both directions
* Poor-accuracy fixes discarded; no distance added while stationary
* Records in the background; trips saved as you go
* Metric and imperial units
* Light, dark and red-on-black night modes, with brightness control
