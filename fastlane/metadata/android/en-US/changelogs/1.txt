First release.

* Two independent trip computers plus a live speed readout
* Distance, average speed, elapsed time and maximum speed per trip
* Average speed from distance over time, with a setting to include or exclude
  time spent stopped
* Regularity mode for events whose regulations forbid an average speed
  computer: each average speed becomes a stopwatch, tap to start or stop and
  hold to clear. Switching mode clears every trip and stopwatch, in both
  directions, so nothing is carried between them
* Poor-accuracy fixes discarded, and no distance clocked up while stationary
* Keeps recording in the background with an ongoing notification
* Trips saved as you go and restored on launch
* Metric and imperial units
* Light, dark and red-on-black night themes, with a brightness control
* Layout adapts to foldables, tablets and split-screen
