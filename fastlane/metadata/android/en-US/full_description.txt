A simple trip computer for walking, cycling and driving. It reads your speed and
distance straight from the GPS receiver — no account, no network, no tracking of
any kind, and nothing leaves your device.

The screen shows two independent trip computers and a live speed readout. Each
trip records distance and average speed, and either can be reset on its own by
tapping it, so you can keep one running for the whole journey and use the other
for a single leg.

Average speed is worked out from distance over time rather than by averaging
speed readings, so it stays honest over a long ride. A setting decides what
happens when you stop: leave "Include stopped time" on and a break at a
checkpoint pulls your average down, which is what you want for an overall
journey average. Switch it off and you get a moving average instead, ignoring
time spent stationary.

Distance only accumulates while the receiver reports real movement, so sitting
still does not slowly clock up phantom miles.

Features:

* Two independent trip computers, each resettable on its own
* Live speed readout
* Overall or moving average speed
* Metric and imperial units
* Light and dark themes
* Screen stays awake while tracking
* Tracking can be switched off entirely to save battery

The app uses location only while it is open. It does not request background
location, and it has no internet permission at all.
