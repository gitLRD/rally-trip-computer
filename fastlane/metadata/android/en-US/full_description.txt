A pair of trip meters and a live speed readout for road rallying, using nothing but your
phone's GPS receiver. Two trips on the dashboard without bolting anything permanent into
the car — and just as useful for cycling, walking, or any drive where you want to know how
far you have actually gone.

No account, no network, no analytics, and no internet permission at all. Nothing leaves
your device.

Regularity mode

Many regularity events forbid a computer that works out average speed. Switch to regularity
mode and every average speed on the screen is replaced by a stopwatch: tap to start or
stop, hold to clear. Changing mode wipes both trips and both stopwatches, in either
direction, so there is nothing carried over from before the switch and nothing to explain
to a scrutineer.

Two trip meters

Each records distance, trip time and maximum speed, and either can be reset on its own with
a tap. Keep one running for the whole event and zero the other at each junction.

In standard mode each also shows average speed, worked out from distance over time rather
than by averaging speed readings, so it stays honest over a long run. "Include stopped
time" decides what happens when you stop: leave it on and a hold-up pulls your average
down, which is what you want against a schedule. Switch it off for a moving average
instead.

Built for the dark

Rallies run at night. Night mode is red on black, which preserves the dark adaptation a
navigator loses to a bright screen, and there is a brightness control in the app so you can
go dimmer than Android normally allows without leaving the screen. Readings are set in
large tabular figures, so the digits do not shuffle sideways as they tick over.

Distance you can trust

Fixes with poor reported accuracy are discarded, and distance only accumulates while the
receiver reports real movement, so sitting at a control does not slowly clock up miles you
never drove. Ground covered while crawling along is still counted.

Features:

* Two independent trip meters, each resettable on its own
* Regularity mode: stopwatches instead of average speed, and a clean slate on every switch
* Live speed readout with a scale you can read at a glance
* Distance, trip time and maximum speed per trip
* Overall or moving average speed
* Metric and imperial units
* Light, dark, and a red-on-black night mode, plus an in-app brightness control
* Keeps recording in the background, with an ongoing notification you can stop from
* Trips are saved as you go and survive the app being closed
* Adapts to foldables, tablets and split-screen

The app uses location only while you have switched tracking on. It does not request
background location permission: recording in the background runs through a foreground
service, which cannot operate without a notification you can see.
