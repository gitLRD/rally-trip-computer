Two independent trip meters and a live speed readout, using nothing but your phone's GPS
receiver. Built for road rallying — a pair of trip meters on the dashboard without bolting
anything permanent into the car — but just as useful for cycling, walking or any drive
where you want to know how far you have actually gone.

No account, no network, no analytics, and no internet permission at all. Nothing leaves
your device.

Each trip records distance, average speed, elapsed time and maximum speed, and either can
be reset on its own by tapping it. Keep one running for the whole journey and use the other
for a single leg between junctions.

Average speed is worked out from distance over time rather than by averaging speed
readings, so it stays honest over a long run. A setting decides what happens when you stop:
leave "Include stopped time" on and a break pulls your average down, which is what you want
against a schedule. Switch it off for a moving average instead.

Distance is guarded carefully. Fixes with poor reported accuracy are discarded, and
distance only accumulates while the receiver reports real movement — so sitting still does
not slowly clock up miles you never drove. Ground covered while crawling along slowly is
still counted.

Features:

* Two independent trip computers, each resettable on its own
* Live speed readout, plus trip time and maximum speed
* Overall or moving average speed
* Metric and imperial units
* Light, dark, and a red-on-black night mode that preserves your night vision
* In-app brightness control
* Keeps recording in the background, with an ongoing notification you can stop from
* Trips are saved as you go and survive the app being closed
* Adapts to foldables, tablets and split-screen

The app uses location only while you have switched tracking on. It does not request
background location permission: recording in the background runs through a foreground
service, which cannot operate without a notification you can see.
